## 1. The Brand & The Logo

### Brand Essence: "اتلموا" (Gather Up)
**Etlamo (اتلموا)** is an authentic Egyptian invitation to assemble, celebrate, and unite in grandeur. It represents the coming together of distinguished families, couples, dignitaries, and delegations in Egypt's most magnificent historic landmarks—from royal châteaux and gilded ballrooms to diplomatic riverfront estates.

### Selected Mark: Concept A — The Sovereign Conclave (المجلس الملكي)
The master mark is an architectural radial roundel. Four sweeping monolithic vaults converge inward in 90° rotational symmetry toward an illuminated 8-pointed star pavilion.
- **Symbolism**: People and delegations converging from North, South, East, and West into one sovereign hall.
- **Dual Reading**: Lateral angles form the Latin capital letter **'E'** (*Etlamo*) and the sweeping calligraphic tail of the Arabic **'ـوا'** (*waw al-jama'a* in اتلموا).
- **Archival Ring**: Framed by a concentric monoline ring reflecting imperial medallions and palace entry portals.

## 3. Clear Space

To maintain visual dignity and clarity, a mandatory clear space zone must surround the logo on all sides.

$$\text{Clear Space} = 1\text{X}$$

Where **1X** equals the diameter of the central 8-pointed star pavilion (64 units on the 256 canvas, or 25% of symbol height).
- No typography, borders, imagery, or competing UI elements may enter this exclusion boundary.
- On banners, headers, and signage, increase margin to **1.5X** or **2X** whenever possible.

## 6. Brand Typography

### Latin Hierarchy
- **Primary Display / Logotype**: *Cormorant Garamond* (Bold / Semi-Bold)
  - Alternatives: *Cinzel*, *Trajan Pro*
  - Used for grand titles, palace names, hero headers, and logo lockup lettering.
- **Interface & Body**: *Inter* / *Plus Jakarta Sans*
  - Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), Semi-Bold (600)
  - Crisp readability for venue specs, guest counts, and pricing tables.
- **Metadata & Protocol Codes**: *JetBrains Mono*
  - Used for protocol badges, reservation numbers, and dates.

### Arabic Hierarchy
- **Primary Display / Calligraphic Mark**: *Amiri* (Bold / 700)
  - Authentic Naskh/Thuluth heritage proportions for "اتلموا" and palatial titles.
- **Interface & Body**: *IBM Plex Sans Arabic*
  - Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), Semi-Bold (600)
  - Fluid, modern Egyptian readability across all UI elements.

## 7. Don'ts (Misuse Prevention)

1. **Do not distort**: Never stretch, compress, or alter the 1:1 aspect ratio.
2. **Do not rotate**: The mark possesses strict 90° rotational symmetry; do not rotate off-axis (e.g. 15° or 45°).
3. **Do not add effects**: No drop shadows, outer glows, bevels, or photographic textures on the mark.
4. **Do not recolor arbitrarily**: Use only approved palette colors (Terracotta, Gold, Onyx, White).
5. **Do not alter lockup proportions**: Do not resize the symbol relative to the typography in the lockups.
6. **Do not recreate the mark with live text**: Always use the provided vector masters.
