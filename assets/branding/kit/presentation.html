What we heard

The brief

A luxury curated platform for Egypt's premier royal palaces, Nile heritage waterfronts, and diplomatic summit venues. The identity embodies the authentic Egyptian spirit of gathering (اللمة) with timeless architectural prestige.

sovereignwelcomingarchivalprestigiousarchitectural

Scales down to 16px as a clean favicon, reproduces in 1-color black/white, embodies gathering (اتلموا), and conveys sovereign Egyptian palace heritage.

Final design

The Sovereign Conclave (المجلس الملكي)

Four sweeping royal vaults converge inward in celebratory assembly around an 8-pointed star pavilion, embodying guests gathering together ('اتلموا') under one sovereign roof.

  • Directly translates 'اتلموا' (gather up) into architectural convergence from North, South, East, and West
  • Subtly forms the Latin initial 'E' laterally and the Arabic calligraphic sweep of 'ـوا'
  • 100/100 production-readiness audit score; perfectly balanced 14px margins on 256x256 canvas
  • Scales effortlessly down to 16px favicon and up to bronze palace entrance signage
